My Love,

I think about our someday a lot — the version of us who made it, who's still this silly and this soft with each other, just further down the road. I want us to actually get there together, and I want to build a whole life out of it with you.

I want to take you to Japan and finally have real wagyu and a proper bowl of ramen with you, no splitting, no sharing, both of us ordering too much. I want to wander Europe with you and eat croissants that ruin every croissant after it, and sourdough still warm from some tiny bakery neither of us can pronounce. I want us to build a beautiful home of our own, and grow something real together — a farm, a business, a life we're actually proud of.

  • Wagyu & ramen in Japan
  • 🥐 Croissants & sourdough in Europe
  • 🏡 A beautiful home, built together
  • 🌾 A successful farming business

None of it means much without you in it, so here's to working for it, saving for it, and someday actually living it — with you, Love.

— always yours, Jerald 💜
